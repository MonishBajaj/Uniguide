MS in Computer Science: Fall 2027 Admission

The following applies to applicants for Fall 2027 entry.

  • Priority deadline (for fellowship and assistantship consideration): December 1, 2026
  • Final deadline: January 15, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time

GRE scores are not required and will not be reviewed.

Applicants whose native language is not English must submit TOEFL iBT scores. For tests taken on or after January 21, 2026, the minimum is a band score of 5.0; for earlier tests, a total of 100.

Tuition for the 2027-2028 academic year is $2,150 per unit. The MS requires 45 units.
