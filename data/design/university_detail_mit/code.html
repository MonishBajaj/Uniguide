About MIT

The Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) is a world-renowned private research university located in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Since its founding in 1861, MIT has played a pivotal role in the development of modern science, engineering, mathematics, and technology.

Known for its rigorous academic culture and "Mens et Manus" (Mind and Hand) philosophy, MIT encourages students to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application. The campus is home to pioneering labs in artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and clean energy, fostering an environment where innovation is the standard.
